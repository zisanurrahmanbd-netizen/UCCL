About UCCL

A recovery partner built from inside the banking sector

UCCL is a Bangladesh-based specialist provider of recovery, collection, consultancy and outsourced field-support services. The firm was founded by three partners with direct, hands-on experience inside leading commercial banks — giving us an operator's understanding of how portfolios, compliance teams and board reporting actually work.

Today we support banks, NBFIs, telecom companies and multinational corporates with disciplined, transparent recovery operations and flexible outsourced support — from written-off loan portfolios to contact point verification and contractual staffing.

  • Team combines strategic leadership with day-to-day operational expertise in recovery and collection
  • Ring-fenced, dedicated teams for each partner institution and portfolio type
  • Documented processes, audit trails and regular management reporting

Direct banking DNA

Our partners have managed recovery and credit functions from within leading commercial banks — the reason institutions trust UCCL with their most sensitive portfolios.

Our Services

End-to-end recovery and outsourced corporate support

Seven service lines, delivered by dedicated teams under a single accountable management structure.

Recovery & Corporate Collection

Structured recovery of bad and non-performing loans — written-off accounts, credit cards, doubtful and general corporate portfolios — through field recovery, negotiation and legal coordination.

Consultancy Services

Advisory support for financial and non-financial institutions on collection strategy, process design, outsourcing frameworks and recovery operations benchmarking.

Contact Point Verification (CPV)

Field verification of applicant employment, business and residential details for banks and financial institutions — accurate, time-bound and fully documented.

Auto Repossession Services

Compliant, coordinated reposession and handover of vehicle and asset security — executed by trained field teams with complete chain-of-custody documentation.

Contractual Staff Support

Screened, trained and supervised manpower deputed to banks, financial institutions and telecom companies — scaled up or down to match seasonal and portfolio volume.

Office Stationery Supply

Reliable procurement and supply of office stationery and consumables to institutional clients, with consistent quality and scheduled delivery.

Sales & Direct-Selling Training

Structured training programs for sales and direct-selling teams — building field capability, conversion discipline and customer-facing professionalism.
